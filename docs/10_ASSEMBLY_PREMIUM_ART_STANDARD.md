# Clue Canvas Premium Artwork Standard — Locked

This is the required visual standard for all new Assembly Mode artwork.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. **Duplicate check first**
   - Before any artwork is generated, compare the proposed phrase against all 565 existing puzzle answers.
   - Check exact wording, accepted-answer variants, and obvious near-duplicate concepts.
   - If an existing puzzle already communicates substantially the same phrase or visual idea, reject it before artwork work begins.

2. **Premium illustration only**
   - No placeholder SVGs, emoji compositions, clip-art-style scenes, or crude code-drawn substitutes may be presented as finished Assembly Mode artwork.
   - Final images must look intentionally art-directed and production-ready.

3. **Visual language**
   - Cinematic storybook/editorial digital illustration.
   - Rich but controlled detail.
   - Strong lighting, depth, atmosphere, texture, and composition.
   - Refined colour grading.
   - Clear focal subject and readable silhouettes.
   - No embedded text, labels, UI, watermarks, or logos.

4. **Puzzle readability**
   - The artwork must remain legible after being divided into puzzle pieces.
   - Include distinct visual zones, edges, landmarks, colour changes, and meaningful detail.
   - Avoid uniform empty areas that create arbitrary difficulty.
   - Avoid excessive visual noise that makes neighbouring pieces indistinguishable.

5. **Clue Canvas relevance**
   - The completed image must communicate a phrase, idiom, or visual idea.
   - Assembly is the mechanic; interpretation is the Clue Canvas payoff.
   - The image must not merely be a pretty generic photograph.

6. **Quality gate**
   - If an image would look cheap beside the strongest premium raster artwork already in Clue Canvas, it does not ship.
   - Mechanic testing may use internal placeholders, but owner-facing and production candidates must meet this standard.
