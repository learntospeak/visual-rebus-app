## Batch 3

1. Every Dog Has Its Day
2. Fortune Favours the Bold
3. Haste Makes Waste
4. If It Ain't Broke, Don't Fix It
5. Ignorance Is Bliss

All five were screened against the current 565 Clue Canvas answers plus the existing Assembly prototype answers. No exact answer collision was found.

## Shared rules

- Standard Clue Canvas puzzle-screen UI only.
- Jigsaw/assembly exists only inside the normal puzzle artwork frame.
- Rotation enabled from the first puzzle.
- No guide or reshuffle controls.
- Answer pattern, compact keyboard, clues, submit and reveal follow production Clue Canvas.
- Artwork must be standalone full-frame art: no UI, no answer text, no headings, no borders, no jigsaw grid baked into the source.
- Premium source art must retain strong visual landmarks for piece slicing.
- Reject any generated artwork that does not clearly infer the intended phrase.

## Puzzle 11 — Every Dog Has Its Day

- Suggested grid: 3×3
- Difficulty target: 5/10
- Core visual: one ordinary dog having one unmistakably special day while the surroundings remain normal.
- Useful scene idea: neighbourhood/farm dog unexpectedly receiving attention, ribbons, treats, celebration or a literal calendar-day cue.
- Must communicate: everyone eventually gets their moment.
- Avoid: generic dog portrait with no “special day” meaning.

## Puzzle 12 — Fortune Favours the Bold

- Suggested grid: 4×3
- Difficulty target: 6/10
- Core visual: two possible paths/choices, with the brave/risky decisive route leading clearly to fortune/reward.
- Strong option: explorer crossing a precarious bridge toward treasure while a timid safer route leads nowhere.
- Must communicate: bold action is rewarded.
- Avoid: generic treasure scene without visible choice/courage.

## Puzzle 13 — Haste Makes Waste

- Suggested grid: 4×4
- Difficulty target: 6/10
- Core visual: someone rushing a task and visibly creating avoidable mess, breakage or wasted materials.
- Strong option: rushed baker/craftsman racing, spilling ingredients/materials and ruining multiple items.
- Must communicate: speed/rushing causes waste.
- Avoid: merely messy workshop with no visible haste.

## Puzzle 14 — If It Ain't Broke, Don't Fix It

- Suggested grid: 5×4
- Difficulty target: 7/10
- Core visual: perfectly functioning machine/object being unnecessarily dismantled or interfered with.
- Strong option: pristine working clock/radio/machine while a person starts taking it apart despite everything working.
- Must communicate: unnecessary fixing of something functional.
- Avoid: actually broken machine.

## Puzzle 15 — Ignorance Is Bliss

- Suggested grid: 5×4
- Difficulty target: 7/10
- Core visual: calm, happy person unaware of obvious trouble/chaos just outside their awareness.
- Strong option: relaxed person reading or resting while behind a wall/window something chaotic unfolds.
- Must communicate: happiness caused by not knowing.
- Avoid: generic peaceful scene without hidden problem.

## Acceptance rule

No item moves to implementation until the source artwork itself clearly communicates the intended meaning without relying on the answer text.
