# Clue Canvas — Phase 6 Content Workflow

## Production unit

Build content in batches of 25 playable puzzles. Never add 25 unfiltered ideas directly to the game.

For each batch:

1. Draft 40 candidate phrases.
2. Remove duplicates, obscure sayings and concepts with debatable answers.
3. Select 30 candidates for visual specification.
4. Build the strongest 25; retain five as replacement candidates.
5. Run automated content validation.
6. Conduct an owner play-through on narrow and large phone layouts.
7. Blind-test every puzzle with at least three people.
8. Approve, revise or reject each puzzle using tester evidence.

## First production batch — puzzles 26–50

- **Chapter:** Between the Lines
- **Difficulty target:** mostly scores 2–4, with no sudden jump from the starter pack
- **Primary mechanics:** above/below, inside/outside, between, repetition, spacing and containers
- **Format balance:** no single mechanic above one-third of the batch
- **Regional target:** globally familiar English first; label AU, UK or US dependence explicitly
- **New-mechanic limit:** introduce at most one unfamiliar idea at a time

## Required record before implementation

- Canonical answer and accepted variants
- Word pattern
- Difficulty score and estimated solve time
- Visual template and exact element specification
- Three progressive clues
- Step-by-step explanation
- Region and originality record
- Artwork source/licence
- Accessibility description
- Draft QA status

## Review gates

### Editorial gate

- The answer is a recognisable phrase.
- The visual produces one clearly strongest answer.
- The concept does not duplicate an existing puzzle.
- The explanation makes every element feel necessary.

### Test gate

- At least two of three blind testers solve an easy puzzle before clue three.
- All testers understand the explanation after solving or revealing.
- Confusing wording is revised once; structurally weak concepts are replaced.
- A puzzle is not marked Approved until three tester results are recorded.

## Scaling milestones

- 50 puzzles: first external content beta
- 100 puzzles: difficulty and repetition audit
- 300 puzzles: performance and retention audit
- 525 puzzles: complete main journey
- 50 additional candidates: reserve and replacement pipeline
