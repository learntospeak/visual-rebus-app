ARTWORK RULES
- Artwork must genuinely communicate the phrase while staying challenging but fair.
- Single-scene by default.
- Multi-frame/split-scene compositions are allowed when one image cannot infer the phrase clearly enough.
- No UI, captions, answer text, logos, borders, watermarks, puzzle-grid overlays, infographic sheets, or mockup screens in source artwork.
- Premium quality, natural anatomy/perspective/object relationships, strong landmarks for puzzle slicing.
- Reject and redo artwork that is visually attractive but semantically weak or misleading.
