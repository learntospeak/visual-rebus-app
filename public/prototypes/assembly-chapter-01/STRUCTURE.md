# Clue Canvas Assembly Mode — Structure v1

## Core loop
1. Reconstruct a premium illustration from shuffled pieces.
2. The completed image becomes a visual clue.
3. Solve the phrase.
4. Earn one fragment toward the chapter master board.
5. After five puzzles, unlock the master assembly.

## Difficulty model
Difficulty is not piece count alone. Each level combines:
- Piece count: 6 / 9 / 12 / 16 / 20+
- Rotation: none → selected pieces → all pieces
- Guide strength: strong silhouette → faint guide → none
- Visual ambiguity: unique regions → repeated colours/textures → near-matching regions
- Rebus difficulty: literal phrase → positional/relational interpretation
- Decoys (future): 0 → 1–3 non-fitting pieces
- Layering (future): flat grid → foreground/background → overlapping transparent pieces

## Puzzle families
1. Classic Assembly — reconstruct a single image.
2. Rotated Assembly — pieces may also need orientation.
3. Layered Rebus — pieces overlap to create meaning.
4. Perspective Assembly — correct alignment creates the clue.
5. Missing Piece — reconstruct enough to infer what is absent.
6. Transformation — completion triggers a second visual state.
7. Master Board — several completed puzzles combine into a larger challenge.

## Chapter pacing
- Level 1: onboarding, 6 pieces, strong visual separation.
- Level 2: 9 pieces, more similar regions.
- Level 3: 12 pieces, partial rotation.
- Level 4: 16 pieces, no guide, stronger ambiguity.
- Level 5: 20 pieces, full rotation, hardest rebus.
- Master: uses the five earned chapter fragments.

## Quality rules
- No walls of text.
- No full-screen rerender on ordinary interaction.
- Every tap gives immediate visual feedback.
- Artwork must be composed as an illustration first, puzzle second.
- Finished image must still require interpretation as a Clue Canvas clue.
- Wrong phrase guesses should be clear but not punitive.
- Mobile-first touch targets and readable piece sizes.
- Progress persists locally only in this prototype.
