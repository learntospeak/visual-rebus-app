Clue Canvas

Delete your account

You can permanently delete your Clue Canvas account and associated cloud progress either inside the app or by sending a request from this page.

Delete inside Clue Canvas

  1. Sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open the profile button or Settings → Your account.
  3. Select Delete account and data.
  4. Read the warning and confirm permanent deletion.

Request deletion without the app

Email us from the same address used for your Clue Canvas account. Put Clue Canvas account deletion in the subject. We may reply to verify that you control the account before deleting it.

Email deletion request

If the button does not open an email app, send the request directly to cluecanvasadmin@gmail.com.

What will be deleted

  • the Supabase authentication account associated with the email address;
  • cloud-saved completions, journey position and star scores;
  • cloud-saved answer reveals, daily history and streak information; and
  • other cloud-progress fields associated with that account.

Deletion is permanent. Local guest progress stored on another device may remain until that app/site data is cleared. An in-app deletion clears progress on the device used to make the deletion.

Timing and retention

In-app deletion is processed immediately when the service is available. Email requests will be handled after ownership verification. Limited security logs, operational backups or request records may remain temporarily where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention or legal compliance.
