Clue Canvas

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 August 2026

Clue Canvas can be played without an account. If you create an optional account, we use your email address and game progress only to sign you in and synchronize your puzzle journey.

Who operates Clue Canvas

Clue Canvas is operated by SilentJZ Studio. Privacy and support questions can be sent to cluecanvasadmin@gmail.com.

Information we collect

If you play without an account, puzzle progress and preferences are stored only in your browser or app storage. This includes completed puzzles, journey position, best star scores, revealed answers, daily-puzzle history, streaks, sound preferences and accessibility settings.

If you create an account, Supabase Authentication processes your email address, password credential, account identifier and authentication-session information. Clue Canvas stores your puzzle progress and its most recent update time in Supabase so it can follow you between devices. Clue Canvas never receives or stores your plaintext password.

Supabase may process technical and security information required to operate its service, such as request timestamps, IP addresses and service logs.

How we use information

  • to create and authenticate an optional player account;
  • to save and synchronize puzzle progress;
  • to restore progress after sign-in or reinstallation; and
  • to maintain the reliability and security of the service.

Information we do not collect

The current release does not contain advertising, payments or analytics SDKs. Clue Canvas does not collect location, contacts, photos, files, camera images, microphone recordings, health information or advertising identifiers. Typed guesses and incorrect answers are not saved. Individual solving time and clues used are shown after a puzzle but are not stored.

Sharing and service providers

Clue Canvas does not sell player information. Supabase is the service provider used for optional authentication, session management and cloud-progress storage. Information may also be disclosed when legally required or when necessary to protect players, the service or others.

Storage, security and retention

Data is transmitted using HTTPS. Cloud-progress records are protected by authentication and database Row Level Security so a player can access only their own progress. No internet service is completely risk-free, but we use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the limited information collected.

Local progress remains until you reset it, clear the app/site data or remove the app data. Cloud account information and progress remain until the account is deleted, subject to limited operational backups, security logs or records that a service provider may retain for legitimate legal and security purposes.

Your choices and deletion

You may play as a guest, sign out at any time, reset local puzzle progress, or permanently delete a signed-in account from the account screen. Deleting the account removes the Supabase authentication account and associated cloud progress. The in-app deletion flow also clears puzzle progress from that device.

If you no longer have access to the app, use the Clue Canvas account-deletion page to request deletion.

Children

Clue Canvas is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly request information from children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child has created an account may contact us to request its deletion.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data handling changes. The current version and update date will remain available on this page.
