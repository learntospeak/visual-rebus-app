Five fresh ways to puzzle.

Premium category ideas for humour, sharing and serious challenge. Try to solve each image before opening its answer. These are concepts only—they are not part of the released game.

Pun IntendedHard · clever

A pun beneath the surface

What familiar phrase does this underwater pair represent?

Reveal answer

Sole mates. They are a pair of sole fish—and they appear to be mates. The less-obvious fish meaning makes this version a much stronger visual pun.

Tiny DetailsMedium · social

Spot the impossible detail

Everything looks normal. One detail breaks reality. Can you find it?

Reveal answer

The spoon’s reflection points the wrong way. This format encourages zooming, debating and sharing with friends.

Australian ThingsMedium · cheeky

Local language, made literal

Which very Australian expression has come to life?

Reveal answer

Flat out like a lizard drinking. A culturally specific pack could turn local slang into highly recognisable visual comedy.

What Am I Looking At?Hard · satisfying

An alien world in your cupboard

What everyday material is shown under extreme magnification?

Reveal answer

Hook-and-loop fastener—commonly known as Velcro. One side contains tiny hooks; the other is a forest of soft loops that the hooks catch.

The Final BossExpert · layered

The clue is hiding in plain sight

The coat, feathers and reflection combine into one familiar expression.

Reveal answer

A blessing in disguise. The ordinary disguise conceals an angelic figure; only the reflected halo and wings expose the layered meaning.
