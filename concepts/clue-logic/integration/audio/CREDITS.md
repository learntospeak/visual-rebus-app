# Scene audio credits

- `door-knock-source.ogg`: **Knocking on Wood or Door**, stephan (26 April 2007), PDSounds recording 198. Public domain, as documented at https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Knocking_on_wood_or_door.ogg . Original download: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/1c/Knocking_on_wood_or_door.ogg .
- `door-knock.wav`: third knock group from that recording, trimmed from 4.36–5.32 seconds, downmixed to mono, normalised and converted to 22,050 Hz PCM. This is a real recorded wooden-door knock.
- `stove-flame.wav`: original generated flame bed, filtered noise with subtle fluctuations, crossfaded into a seamless loop.
- `rapid-boiling.wav`: original generated bubbling bed with overlapping irregular resonant bubble bursts, crossfaded into a seamless loop. These two loops are designed effects, not field recordings.

All three shipped WAVs are local assets. No third-party service is contacted during playback. Voice and subtitles were removed at the owner's request.
